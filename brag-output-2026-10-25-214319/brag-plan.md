# Brag Plan: Jarvis Control Center

## What is this app?

Jarvis is a local-first voice assistant that turns natural-language requests into answers or browser actions, powered by Ollama and Gemma 4 E2B.

## The angle

Treat a personal assistant like a quiet mission-control system: the interface wakes, listens, reasons, and executes without leaving the browser.

## Hook

“Your browser just got a chief of staff.” A dark control-room interface powers on around the Jarvis orb.

## Key moments

- The orb shifts from standby to listening while a voice command types in.
- Friday Brain resolves the request into either an answer or a tool action.
- A compact activity rail shows browser automation, local AI, and synced state moving in sequence.

## Outro / punchline

“Local intelligence. Useful hands.” Finish on JARVIS CONTROL CENTER with “Standing by.”

## User flow worth showing

Voice input → Gemma 4 E2B reasoning through Ollama → browser/tool action → spoken or visible result.

## Tone

- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: premium sci-fi product film, restrained and precise
- Interpretation: slow confident reveals, minimal copy, cyan state light, no generic SaaS claims.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity

- Background: `#04060c`
- Accent: `#45d8ff`
- Text: `#e6edf6`
- Display font: system grotesk / Inter-like sans
- Body font: Geist Mono-like monospace
- Strongest visual: the reactive central orb and glass control panels.

## Share copy

Jarvis Control Center: a local-first voice assistant that listens, reasons with Gemma 4 E2B, and gets useful browser work done.

## Audio direction

- Role: restrained upbeat electronic bed with interface accents
- Music: bundled Happy Beats / Business Moves track, low under the UI
- Music treatment: fade in at 0.2s, accent the orb reveal and final lockup, fade out over the final second
- Music cue guidance: use bundled cue metadata; target strong cues near 2.4s, 8.2s, and 18.4s
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let the orb glow breathe with the bed
- SFX posture: sparse and precise
- Restraint rule: no trailer booms or noisy glitch effects.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Boot sequence — 3.5s

Black-blue grid. JARVIS wordmark resolves; the orb core ignites.

Sequential/interaction: wordmark, status line, and orb appear in order.
Audio intent: quiet power-up.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — Listen — 4.5s

Main glass dashboard appears. A command types in: “Open YouTube and play Aaya Sher.” The orb changes to LISTENING.

Sequential/interaction: typed command and listening state.
Audio intent: subtle UI ticks.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — Reason — 4.5s

The command becomes a clean decision rail: “FRIDAY BRAIN / TOOL SELECTED / youtube_play”. Gemma 4 E2B and Ollama appear as quiet technical labels.

Sequential/interaction: three status rows reveal one by one.
Audio intent: one restrained confirmation accent.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — Execute — 4.0s

The browser action card moves from QUEUED to EXECUTING to COMPLETE while the right-side activity rail updates.

Sequential/interaction: status transition and activity cards.
Audio intent: crisp completion tick.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — Lockup — 3.5s

Full interface settles. JARVIS CONTROL CENTER, “Local intelligence. Useful hands.” and STANDING BY remain readable.

Sequential/interaction: final line fades in after the lockup.
Audio intent: music resolves cleanly.
Transition mood: soft → end.

**Music mood:** polished electronic.
**Audio summary:** a light, precise pulse that follows the system from boot to useful action, then leaves room for the final lockup.
